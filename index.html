Start with 211.

In many parts of the United States, 211 connects people with local referrals for housing, food, utility bills, and other essential needs. Availability varies by area.

How HopeScout works

Finding help can feel overwhelming. We aim to make the first steps easier.

STEP 01

Choose your need

Search or browse a category to find a relevant starting point.

STEP 02

Check the details

Review eligibility, location, deadlines, and requirements on the official website.

STEP 03

Apply safely

Use official application channels and never pay someone who promises guaranteed grant approval.

Protect yourself: HopeScout does not award grants or decide eligibility. Be cautious of anyone demanding upfront fees, gift cards, passwords, or bank login details in exchange for “free money.” Always verify offers with the organization running the program.
